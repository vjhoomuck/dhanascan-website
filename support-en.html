DhanaScan Support

Simple answers to help you use DhanaScan with cards, workbooks and sounds.

How do I use DhanaScan?

Open DhanaScan and start immediately with the built-in virtual cards. The child scans a card, listens, repeats, then continues learning with workbooks, challenges, riddles and animated episodes. Printed cards remain optional and are especially useful for physically building words.

How do I print cards and workbooks?

Cards, badges and workbooks are available in the Resources section. They can be printed for personal family or educational use.

Sound problem

Check the device volume, silent mode, and the audio settings on your phone or tablet.

Camera problem

Check that camera permission is enabled for DhanaScan in your device settings.

Download DhanaScan

The app is available on iOS and Android.
