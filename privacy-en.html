Privacy policy

Privacy

DhanaScan is designed to provide a simple, reassuring educational experience adapted to children.

No advertising

DhanaScan does not contain advertising aimed at children.

No personal data collection

The app does not collect children’s personal data and does not create child profiles.

No child account

No child account is required to use the main features.

Main features work offline

DhanaScan’s main features can be used without an internet connection, including card recognition, sound playback and downloaded workbooks.

Camera

The camera is used only to recognise DhanaScan educational cards. Images stay on the device and are never sent to a server.

No images sent

Scanned images are not sent to a server.

Resources and content

Paid resources and YouTube episodes should be used with the agreement and supervision of a parent or responsible adult.

All resources provided by DhanaScan are protected by copyright and remain the property of DhanaScan.
